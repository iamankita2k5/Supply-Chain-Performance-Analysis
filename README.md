# 📊 Supply Chain Performance Analysis

## 🚀 Project Overview

Supply Chain Performance Analysis is an interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze and monitor different areas of supply chain operations.

The dashboard provides data-driven insights into **sales, inventory, supplier performance, production, logistics, and product quality**. It helps identify high-performing areas, operational problems, cost-related issues, and opportunities for improvement.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The main objective of this project is to use data analytics and visualization to understand supply chain performance and support better business decision-making.

The dashboard helps businesses monitor key performance indicators, identify trends, reduce operational costs, improve inventory management, and maintain product quality.

---

## ❓ Key Business Questions

- Which products and locations generate the highest revenue?
- Which products have the highest stock levels and order quantities?
- Which suppliers have better production and lead-time performance?
- Which products contribute the most to total production?
- Which transportation modes have higher shipping costs?
- Which locations and products have higher defect rates?
- Where can supply chain costs and operational delays be reduced?

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Skincare** contributes the highest revenue and production volume among the product categories.
- **Mumbai and Kolkata** are among the strongest locations in terms of revenue.
- Inventory levels vary across product types and locations, making regular stock monitoring important.
- Supplier performance differs in terms of production volume and lead time.
- **Air transportation** has a relatively higher shipping cost compared with other transportation modes.
- Some locations and products show higher defect rates and require additional quality monitoring.

---

## ✅ Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing products such as **Skincare** while maintaining sufficient inventory.
- Improve sales strategies in lower-performing locations.
- Regularly compare suppliers based on production, cost, lead time, and defect rate.
- Optimize transportation routes and modes to reduce logistics costs.
- Monitor inventory levels to avoid both stockouts and excess inventory.
- Improve quality control in locations and products with higher defect rates.
